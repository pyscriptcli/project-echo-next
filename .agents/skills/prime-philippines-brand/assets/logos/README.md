# PRIME Logos

The **full logo** is the PRIME wordmark + "Philippines" + the double-chevron mark.
These are the official PRIME artwork files, embedded here as transparent PNGs.

## Files (bundled)

| File | Variant | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| `blue_prime_logo.png` | Blue | Light / Warm White (`#FFFCFB`) backgrounds — primary for light/print |
| `white_prime_logo.png` | White | PRIME Blue `#003366` backgrounds |

## Selection rule

PRIME has two backgrounds, so there are two cases and no judgment call.

- Warm White `#FFFCFB` → **blue** logo (`blue_prime_logo.png`).
- PRIME Blue `#003366` → **white** logo (`white_prime_logo.png`).

Gray and Deep Ink backgrounds are retired, so nothing should ever place a logo on one.

## Notes
- Both files have transparent backgrounds and are ~2400px on the long edge — crisp for
  decks, docs, and web. Higher-resolution originals exist if you need print at large scale.
- Do not recolor outside the two approved variants; do not place blue-on-dark or
  white-on-light; do not stretch, rotate, or add effects. Keep generous clear space.
