# PRIME Color System — "Palette of Sovereign Intelligence"

**Philosophy:** PRIME Gold crowns every premium moment. Blue is the foundation of
authority. Yellow energizes. Gray grounds the type.

**Surface rule (read this first):** only two colors go behind content — PRIME Blue
`#003366` and PRIME Warm White `#FFFCFB`. Gray and Deep Ink are text colors, not
backgrounds. The single documented exception is data-viz print, which is forced to
`#FFFFFF`.

## Primary colors

### PRIME Blue — `#003366` · rgb(0, 51, 102)
**Foundation · Authority.** The same frequency as banks and Supreme Court seals,
inescapably authoritative. This is the **primary** color and the dominant field color
across the system, and it's the only dark surface PRIME uses.
- Use for: foundational elements, authority signals, institutional contexts, primary
  surfaces, headings, the dominant brand color block.
- Use for: any moment that wants weight and contrast. Where a dark theme would once
  have called for Gray or Deep Ink, use a Blue field with Warm White type on it.

### PRIME Gold — `#C9A84C` · rgb(201, 168, 76)
**Apex Accent · Distinction.** Warm yellow-gold calibrated to connote earned success,
not ostentation. Reserved **exclusively for accents.**
- Allowed: accents, rule lines, signature moments, key numerals/highlights.
- Forbidden: dominant field color, large background areas, body fills.
- Rule: **never the dominant field color.** If gold occupies more than a thin accent
  area, it's wrong.

### PRIME Warm White — `#FFFCFB` · rgb(255, 252, 251)
**Softness · Clarity.** Warmth for light backgrounds, cards, and breathing space.
Intentional, never sterile. Use **instead of pure white** for all brand/marketing
surfaces. Together with Blue this is one of the two permitted background colors, and it
also carries type on Blue fields.

## Secondary & neutral colors

### PRIME Yellow / Amber — `#FFBF00` · rgb(255, 191, 0)
**Energy · Motion.** Amber — the arrow mark itself. Used within infographics and data
visualization to signal energy and motion. Distinct from PRIME Gold: Yellow is brighter
and is the *motion* accent; Gold is the *premium* accent.

### PRIME Gray / Black Pearl — `#181D1E` · rgb(24, 29, 30)
**Depth · Sophistication.** Warmer than pure black. **Ink only.** Body copy, hairlines,
footnotes, and table rules on Warm White surfaces. It exists so nothing has to be set in
pure black. Do not fill a background with it.

### PRIME Deep Ink — `#0C0C0E` · rgb(12, 12, 14)
**Ultra Dark · Ink only.** The deepest text weight, for the rare case where Gray reads
too soft against Warm White. Not a background, not a dark theme, not a website field.

## Usage discipline

- Backgrounds are **PRIME Blue or PRIME Warm White**. Nothing else.
- PRIME Gold is an **accent only**, never a dominant field color.
- PRIME Blue is the **foundation of authority** and the dominant color.
- PRIME Yellow is reserved for data visualization and the arrow mark.
- PRIME Warm White replaces pure white for warmth.
- PRIME Gray and PRIME Deep Ink are **text colors**, and they're the reason nothing needs
  to be set in pure black.

## Recommended pairings

Two surfaces, and that's the whole set.

| Surface | Background | Text | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light / print / body pages | Warm White `#FFFCFB` | PRIME Blue `#003366` for headings, PRIME Gray `#181D1E` for body | Gold rule lines `#C9A84C` |
| Authority block / hero / dark moments / web | PRIME Blue `#003366` | Warm White `#FFFCFB` | Gold `#C9A84C` |

## Do not

- Do not use PRIME Gray or PRIME Deep Ink as a background. Blue and Warm White are the
  only two.
- Do not build a dark theme out of anything but PRIME Blue.
- Do not use PRIME Gold as a background fill.
- Do not use pure black `#000000` for text — use PRIME Gray or PRIME Deep Ink.
- Do not use pure white `#FFFFFF` for brand surfaces — use PRIME Warm White.
  (Data-viz print backgrounds are the documented exception.)
